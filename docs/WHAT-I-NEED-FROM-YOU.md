## 2 · Decisions I made for you — tell me if any are wrong

**The wordmark is `HARDHIK`, not `HARDHIK KUMAR`.** Seven letters, exactly like
`GIREESH`, which means every measured percentage in the hero layout still holds
and the opening needed no re-fitting at all. `HARDHIK KUMAR` would need the
whole hero geometry re-solved. Say the word if you want it anyway.

**I merged the two CVs, favouring the Talent Hub one.** The two PDFs tell
slightly different stories — `new_june_4` reads as a software developer moving
into analytics, `Talent_hub_resume` reads as a business analyst who happens to
have shipped code. The second is the stronger, more specific document, so the
site is built on it: the Nexgen role is described as requirements and
specification work, and the capstone is the headline project. If the first is
the one you are actually sending out, tell me and I will re-pitch it.

**I dropped the cigarette.** It runs through the hero clip and the footer in the
original. On a portfolio aimed at UK business-analyst roles it reads as a
liability, so both prompts now put a laptop in your hand instead. Easy to put
back — the line is in FLOW-PROMPTS.md section A.

**I kept the red-and-black grade.** It is bold for a BA portfolio, but it is
also the entire design system — every shader, every rim light, every reveal is
built on that palette. Changing it means regenerating all six assets *and*
retuning five shader files. Worth it only if you actively dislike it.

**Park Regis is on the timeline, not in the work deck.** The night-audit
reconciliation appears as project card 11, *"Reconciled Before Dawn"* — it is
genuinely relevant (investigating discrepancies to a deadline) but I have not
given it top billing. Remove it if you would rather the deck were all analysis.
